# Worlds 2025 Format Notes

This simulation assumes Riot retained the competitive structure that debuted at Worlds 2023 and was reiterated for the 2024 season, as documented by Wikipedia based on the official broadcast guide for Worlds 2024: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_League_of_Legends_World_Championship>.

* **Play-In** – The lower seeds from the LCK and LPL contest two qualifier series. KT Rolster and Top Esports play a best-of-five to determine which team receives the higher Swiss seeding (Pool 2) and which enters Pool 3. Invictus Gaming and T1 play a best-of-five where the winner claims the final Swiss berth and the loser is eliminated. Only one of Invictus Gaming or T1 progresses to Swiss.
* **Swiss Stage** – Sixteen teams play a five-round Swiss bracket. Round 1 pairs Pool 1 teams with Pool 3 teams and has Pool 2 teams face one another while avoiding same-region clashes. Subsequent rounds match teams with identical records, allow intra-region matchups, and prevent rematches. Advancement/elimination matches (any series that could give a team a third win or a third loss) are best-of-three; all other Swiss matches are best-of-one. Teams with three wins advance, while those with three losses are eliminated.
* **Knockout Stage** – Eight qualified teams are drawn into a single-elimination bracket. The 3–0 Swiss teams are placed on opposite sides and face 3–2 teams in the quarterfinals. The remaining quarterfinal pairings are randomly drawn. All knockout matches are best-of-five.

These assumptions are used to contextualize bracket generation in the accompanying simulation report.
